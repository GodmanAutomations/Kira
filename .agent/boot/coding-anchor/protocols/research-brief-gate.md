# Research Brief Gate

Use this gate before acting on external facts, current APIs, policy, pricing,
standards, or recommendations that could drift.

## Gate

1. State the research question.
2. Identify what would make the answer stale or risky.
3. Gather primary sources before secondary sources.
4. Cross-check important claims across independent sources.
5. Mark unsupported or single-source claims as unverified.
6. Record confidence and what would change the answer.
7. Link the sources used in user-facing answers.

## Kira Boundary

Generated research briefs live under `research-briefs/` and are ignored by Git.
They are working notes, not public docs, unless Stephen explicitly asks to
preserve one.
