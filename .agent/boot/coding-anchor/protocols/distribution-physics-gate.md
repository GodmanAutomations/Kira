# Distribution Physics Gate

Use this gate before launch plans, acquisition strategy, marketing campaigns,
channel selection, or building features whose value depends on an audience
finding them.

## Gate

1. Name the product, offer, audience, and desired action.
2. Run PMOD:
   - Problem: does it hurt enough to pay or switch?
   - Market: are there enough reachable people with the pain?
   - Operations: can it be delivered profitably?
   - Distribution: can those people be reached at sustainable cost?
3. Identify the primary channel and backup channel.
4. Name the channel tax: cash, time, relationships, platform risk, or equity.
5. Check CAC, LTV, gross margin, payback, and confidence.
6. Check Four Fits:
   - market-product
   - product-channel
   - channel-model
   - model-market
7. Choose one testable distribution experiment.
8. Define kill criteria, evidence needed, and next slice.

## Red Flags

- assuming the audience will appear after the product ships
- choosing a channel because it is familiar rather than structurally fit
- paid acquisition without CAC/LTV/payback assumptions
- organic strategy without time budget and compounding cadence
- platform dependency without backup channel
- fixing copy when the channel itself is broken

## Kira Boundary

Generated distribution plans live under `distribution-plans/` and are ignored
by Git. This gate helps prepare strategy; it does not launch campaigns, spend
money, publish assets, or contact prospects unless Stephen explicitly asks for
that action.
