# Consiglieri Gate

Use this gate before high-stakes meetings, partnerships, collaborations,
negotiations, trust decisions, or emotionally loaded responses.

## Gate

1. Name the situation, counterparty, desired outcome, and stakes.
2. Pryce Test:
   - Is the deal too neat?
   - Am I assuming mutual interest?
   - Am I ignoring ego, desperation, opportunism, or institutional incentives?
3. Exit Test:
   - Can I walk away quickly?
   - Are there sunk-cost, shame, reputation, financial, or access handcuffs?
   - Do they need me at least as much as I need them?
4. Information-security check:
   - What have I already revealed?
   - What should stay unsaid?
   - What leverage would be lost if I answer too much?
5. Time-pressure check:
   - Is urgency real, externally verifiable, and proportionate?
   - If urgency is artificial, pause.
6. Blast-radius audit:
   - Who else is affected?
   - What becomes permanent?
   - What domains could cross-contaminate?
   - What is the smallest safe next step?
7. Incentive read:
   - What is each party optimizing for?
   - What would they do if no one was watching?
8. Adult-to-Adult rewrite:
   - detect emotional hook
   - strip emotional payload from operational payload
   - respond only to the operational payload
9. Decide: proceed, pause, narrow scope, require commitment, or exit.

## Red Flags

- pressure to decide before basic checks
- request for information, access, work, or reputation before commitment
- pushback against normal documentation
- flattering offer with unclear economics
- emotional hook that tries to make a boundary feel rude
- unclear exit
- two or more Vibe Veto signals

## Kira Boundary

Generated trust reviews live under `trust-reviews/` and are ignored by Git.
This gate helps prepare judgment and communication; it does not contact anyone,
send messages, sign agreements, or take live-world action unless Stephen
explicitly asks for that action.
