# Power Inversion Gate

Use this gate before a negotiation, quote, partnership decision, or information
release where Stephen could lose leverage.

## Gate

1. Name the deal and the valuable asset at risk.
2. Map Stephen's BATNA.
3. Map the counterparty BATNA.
4. Identify who invests first under the current plan.
5. Require a commitment device before releasing leverage.
6. Set the walk-away condition.
7. Draft the next move in plain, calibrated language.

## Red Flags

- They want information, access, or work before any commitment.
- Their promise costs them nothing but Stephen's preparation costs real time.
- The price anchor comes from the counterparty, not structural value.
- Stephen would have no recourse if they bypass, ghost, or extract the asset.
- "Just send it first" is doing the work of a missing contract.

## Athena Boundary

Generated negotiation notes live under `negotiations/` and are ignored by Git.
This gate protects leverage; it does not authorize payments, contracts, or
external contact unless Stephen has asked for that action.
